Cloud ice mixing ratio wrt dry air (from wet air and total water)
==================================================================

* **Description**: Produces cloud ice mixing ratio wrt dry air from cloud ice mixing ratio wrt moist air and condensed water and total water mixing ratio wrt moist air and condensed water.
* **Name**: CloudIceMixingRatioWrtDryAir_B
* **Variable produced**: cloud_ice_mixing_ratio_wrt_dry_air
* **Input Variables**: cloud_ice_mixing_ratio_wrt_moist_air_and_condensed_water, total_water_mixing_ratio_wrt_moist_air_and_condensed_water
* **Trajectory Variables**: cloud_ice_mixing_ratio_wrt_moist_air_and_condensed_water, total_water_mixing_ratio_wrt_moist_air_and_condensed_water
* **Number of Levels**: Same as cloud_ice_mixing_ratio_wrt_moist_air_and_condensed_water
* **FunctionSpace**: Same as cloud_ice_mixing_ratio_wrt_moist_air_and_condensed_water
* **hasTLAD**: True
* **hasNL**: True
* **Required Configuration Variables**: None
* **Optional Parameters**: None
